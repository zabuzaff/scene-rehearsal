# Scene Rehearsal
The owner inspects and approves authored simulation behavior before implementation. Agents publish and revise through the CLI. The workbench prioritizes the scene, a shallow timeline and a contextual inspector. The After Hours studio theme uses graphite surfaces, pale blue controls, Syne headings and DM Sans for review text. The neutral 3D office keeps its own asset colors. The project is desktop-first with stacked inspection on narrow screens.
