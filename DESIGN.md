# Scene Rehearsal design

Scene Loom’s local After Hours studio is the visual reference. Use its graphite surfaces, pale blue accent, Syne Variable display hierarchy and DM Sans Variable controls and body text. Keep the playback mark as the Rehearsal identity.

The main surface is a studio for operating the rehearsal. The revision picker belongs in the wordmark bar. The scene title and scenario tools frame a canvas that fills the available workspace, with a shallow playback shelf and a raised inspector to the right. At narrow widths the inspector follows the stage and compared views stack when there is insufficient room.

The library uses Loom’s centered composition and scene-card title treatment for revision navigation. Keep hover, focus and selected states legible and retain text labels for revision, playback and review states. Model colors belong to the scene.
